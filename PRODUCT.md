# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript static build, deployed to GitHub Pages as an installable PWA. Chosen because Minh asked for GitHub delivery and the lowest token cost; there is no server, and audio/video are pre-rendered at build time.

## Users

One player: **Gia Huy Trần**, Minh's boyfriend, a young Vietnamese jellyfish-tank keeper at an aquarium café in Ho Chi Minh City (since 22/09/2026). He plays on his phone in short breaks and in the evening after the gym, roughly one hour a day in total. His TOEIC is about 335-405 today; his first target is 450-500 for graduation, then 800. He only plays Liên Quân and is anxious about making mistakes. Sources: plan/01-learner-profile.md, zalo-private/huy-persona.md.

Secondary: **Minh**, the giver and game master, who writes the love letters and clues and wants to see Huy progress.

## Product Purpose

A daily TOEIC learning game that moves Huy from ~400 to 500, then 800, with real exam-format practice (Listening Parts 1-4, Reading Parts 5-7, vocabulary, grammar), while making him feel loved. Success = Huy plays most days and his timed boss scores rise.

## Positioning

It is made for one person: his real job (jellyfish keeper), his real jellyfish photos and videos, and real moments from his chats with Minh, turned into English lessons, inside a love story with a twist. No generic TOEIC app can copy that.

## Operating Context

- Phone, portrait, often one-handed, in a bright café or late at night in bed.
- Sessions of 20 minutes (lunch break) and 40 minutes (evening).
- Vietnamese UI with a VI ⇄ EN toggle; exam content always in English.
- Audio must sound human, in TOEIC accents (US, UK, AU, CA).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Daily dive loop: SRS warm-up → listening → Part 5/6 → Part 7 → new words → story fragment.
- Oxygen (lives), light (points), bank-or-push, weekly timed bosses, Liên Quân-style ranks tied to a forecast score, a Jellydex collection, a mistake log.
- Season 1 = 21 days of content; Seasons 2-5 follow (plan/07-roadmap-to-800.md).
- No copyrighted test-prep material in the repo. Personal chat content only after Minh approves it, and kept out of a public repo.
- Progress is stored locally on Huy's phone (no account).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Sứa Đêm · Midnight Jelly** (recommended in the plan; Minh asked for a jellyfish name).
- Voice: warm older-partner "anh/em" tone, playful, with Huy's own texting style for the sidekick ("hihi", "dọ", ":33").
- Must exceed the quality of Minh's earlier games *Rắn công sở* and *Delivered ✓✓*.
- Minh asked for: colorful, fun, engaging, about love, many clues and tips from Minh.

## Evidence on Hand

- 16 videos and 13 photos of real jellyfish Huy shared: `zalo-private/media/` (private; not for a public repo without Minh's yes).
- Learner profile, research, curriculum and story: `plan/`.
- No real TOEIC score after 04/08/2026 exam yet. No recorded voice letters from Minh yet.

## Product Principles

1. **Feel loved before feeling tested.** Every session opens and closes with warmth.
2. **Mistakes are safe.** Losing is gentle and always shows the way back.
3. **Real exam, real progress.** Format, timing and difficulty mirror TOEIC; the score forecast is honest.
4. **His world, not a textbook's.** Jellyfish, the aquarium, Liên Quân ranks, his and Minh's moments.
5. **Small daily wins.** A 20-minute session must still feel complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Vietnamese diacritics everywhere; captions/transcripts for all audio after answering; reduced-motion mode; 44px touch targets; readable at arm's length on a phone at night.
