Scope: the whole game app (mobile-first web), mode Operate. Player: Huy (jellyfish keeper, TOEIC ~400). Job: finish today's dive (20-60 min) and feel loved.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The game is Huy's own jellyfish room after closing time: every screen is a lit tank beside its museum placard. It refuses the category default of a cartoon quiz app with a progress bar and confetti.
OWN-WORLD: Tank-room indigo ground (#0A0D29 to #141A4A). Color lives only inside lit tanks and plates: moon-jelly ice blue (listening), coral (reading), flame orange (streak), magenta-rose (Minh, love), spotted-lagoon white. Placards are cool white label stock with black type, a specimen number, Latin italic name, habitat bar. Rounded tank windows (28px), placards square-cornered (6px).
STORY: Huy opens the app, sees tonight's tanks waiting with glowing jellyfish, a note from Minh pinned on the glass; he works the tanks (questions), each answer makes a jellyfish pulse or doze; he ends with a story fragment and a new species in the Jellydex.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height tank window (60% of the viewport) playing Huy's real jellyfish footage, today's Day number and rank (Liên Quân ladder) on a placard at the bottom-left, Minh's pinned note on the glass top-right, one primary button "Bắt đầu ca trực" at thumb height, oxygen and light gauges as a slim strip.
FORM: Aquarium species placard (my grounded list #4: tank + museum label). Seed 54af3c6f. Raises: from star atlas, glow magnitude encodes mastery; from lexicon, guide words at the head ("Ngày 3 · Part 5 · 4/10") and color quarantined in plates; from ticket wallet, mistakes stamp and never vanish; from vertical feed, one question owns the viewport with the next one a snap away; from console, quitting a dive isolated from answer controls; from type specimen, the VI/EN toggle morphs every label at once.
SIGNATURE: the jellyfish pulse (bell contraction with tentacle lag) on correct answers; dozing drift on wrong ones.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
