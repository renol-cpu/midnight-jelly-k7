# 05 · Curriculum: the first 3 weeks (Season 1)

> The teacher's seat. Start point from [01-learner-profile](01-learner-profile.md): about 335-405, Part 5 tenses/passive strong, word form, collocations and no/not weak, real listening untested. Season 1 target: a timed mock at **≥ 450**, with a stretch to 500. Weeks 4+ are Season 2, toward 600 → 700-800.

## Daily one-hour loop (the "dive")

| Min | Block | What happens | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6 | **Hồi sinh sứa** (SRS warm-up) | 10-15 cards that are due: words, collocations and yesterday's misses | Dim jellyfish glow again |
| 6-22 | **Radio** (Listening) | 2 sets of 10 real-audio items for the week's parts; the second set runs at exam speed | Oxygen; ×1.5 inside TOEIC time |
| 22-36 | **Nhật ký rách** (Part 5/6) | A 90-second theory card first (he asked for theory first), then 10 items, scored, then 10 more | 10/10 opens a bonus chest |
| 36-50 | **Đèn pin** (Part 7) | 1-2 passages: read the questions first, then use the flashlight to scan | Flashlight trains skim/scan |
| 50-56 | **Sứa mới** (vocab) | 12-15 new TOEIC Service List words through 2-3 new jellyfish | Jellydex grows |
| 56-60 | **Mảnh truyện** | Story fragment (voiced audio, EN with VI subs) + a "lời nhắn của anh" | Cliffhanger |

There is a 20-minute "short dive" option (warm-up + one main block + story) for busy days, which still lights the day's lantern. Day 7 of each week is the **boss** plus a light review, so the week is 6 learning days and 1 test day.

## Teaching rules baked into the game

1. **Theory card first**, one rule per card, with a Vietnamese example and an English example, under 90 seconds. Then practice.
2. **Score every 10 items** and explain every miss: why the right answer is right, why his choice is a trap, and the one-line pattern to remember (his favorite format).
3. **Never trust a single answer key.** Items are written to a template and checked twice (an automated validator plus a second-pass review), since wrong ChatGPT keys hurt him before.
4. **85% rule.** Adaptive difficulty keeps live accuracy around 80-88%: two misses in a row step the difficulty down, and five hits step it up.
5. **Real audio only for listening.** No text transcripts until after answering, then the transcript appears with the key words highlighted.
6. **The score estimate comes from bosses only**, mapped to the 5-495 scale per section (see [02-research](02-research.md)), and shown honestly against 450.
