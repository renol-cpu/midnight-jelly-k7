## 4. Recurring mistakes (paraphrased)
1. `no` + noun vs `not` + adj/verb: "There were not applicants" (should be "no").
2. Choosing a word that "feels familiar" (keyword matching) in Part 7 instead of what the text says.
3. Collocations: improve production/productivity; compatible *with*; staffed *by*; depend *on*.
4. Word form: noun vs adj slot; asked "why not V3 after and?" - trusts parallel form over slot.
5. Wish: would make vs had made; forgets past markers ("last night" means had + V3).
6. Relative: whom vs whose (pick whose when a noun follows).
7. Subject-verb: "rarely arrives" (picked past tense), Neither...nor nearest subject.
8. Typos in his own answer strings (e.g. "1d" for 10d, "7s", skipped numbers) - he rushes, so some "errors" are input errors.
9. Complains when ChatGPT gives no explanation ("ko giai thich em sai cau nao").

## 7. How he learns
- Pace: **daily, late night (some at ~1am)**, up to 100+ Part 5 items/day; 10-30 item blocks. Tends to say "mai on" then slack; loses focus some days ("nay em ko tap trung duoc").
- Motivation: graduation deadline (IUH) + fear of 405 again; likes being told "gan nhu 100%", hearts in score; "hu hon" relief when scored higher than feared. Wants shortcuts ("on cap toc", "gap la an diem").
- Prefers: theory **before** exercises ("chua co ly thuyet ma dua bai tap roi a"), formulas, mini tests in his own format "1b 2c 3a", block scoring of 10, then explanation of each wrong answer.
- Stays on one topic per chat ("khung chat nay minh hoc pronoun thoi"). Moves on only when he scores well.
- Language: Vietnamese chat, abbreviations (e, a, ko, r, lun, hong, hic, kkk), casual and playful, tests the tutor ("em thach"); corrects the tutor when an item is wrong (c3 - and the tutor thanked him).

## 8. Coaching voice that worked

## 9. What worked / what didn't
- Worked: topic mini tests + immediate scoring; formula one-liners (wish + S + would + V); tables of keyword -> answer; recap of "what you mastered"; mixed tests with hidden topic.
- Didn't: answers without explanation; practice before theory; 20+ hard items when tired; ChatGPT's own errors in answer keys (c3 item 10, c6 item 27); listening via text (no audio); ChatGPT being over-optimistic vs the 335 mock; his "checklists" mostly grammar while Reading/Listening raw scores lag.

## 10. Implications for game design
- **Start difficulty**: Part 5 easy-medium tier (he is ~80% on drilled grammar) but start Listening, Part 6, Part 7 and vocabulary at beginner-intermediate (A2). Do not hand him ETS-hard blocks first.
- **Priorities (order)**: (1) real audio Listening Parts 1-4 with transcripts after; (2) Part 5 collocations + word form + prepositions; (3) Part 6 connectors / insertion; (4) Part 7 short single passages with anti-trap questions; (5) Vocabulary SRS (business words, EN-VN). Grammar already drilled: tenses, passive, wish keep as spaced review only.
- **Mechanics**: 10-item blocks, per-block score screen, explanation per wrong answer ("why other options are wrong"), theory card shown before each new topic, "to phao" cheat sheets, stage gates at 9/10, daily streak and a 20-30 min daily quest (he studies at night).
- **Fix input noise**: tap answers, no typed "1b 2c" strings.
- **Adaptive**: track errors per skill tag (no/not, wish tense, collocation, noun/adj slot, keyword trap) and resurface them.
- **Tone**: Vietnamese UI, anh/em-friendly encouraging copy, light humor, no shaming; show estimated TOEIC score band using the mock conversion (66 -> 335; 85-90 -> ~450) so progress to 450 is visible.
- **Data caveat**: sampled, accuracy figures are estimates from the strings and ChatGPT's own scoring.
